checks every 3 minutes

questions

FAQ

is it really completely free
yes. no trial, no premium tier, no credit card, no "upgrade to pro". the full product, forever, for $0. ahmed built it for himself and decided to share it.
what's the catch
there genuinely isn't one. no data collection, no ads, no upsell. it's a chrome extension that runs locally in your browser. ahmed makes money from other projects.
will it send duplicate messages
no. it checks existing DM threads before every send. if you already messaged someone manually or the bot sent before, it skips them. only first messages ever.
does it work if i don't follow them back
yes. if someone follows you, you can DM them whether you follow back or not. the message lands in their inbox or message requests.
will it get my account flagged
it has built in safety: random delays, daily limits, auto pause on warnings. keep your daily limit at 10 to 15 and you'll be fine. we still recommend using a secondary account for peace of mind.
does it collect my data
no. zero. nothing. no server, no cloud, no tracking. everything runs in your browser. we literally could not access your data if we tried.
