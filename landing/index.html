what it actually does

🧠

watches your new followers

checks every 20 minutes while your instagram tab is open. no background process eating your cpu.

👋

types a welcome DM like a human would

character by character, random delays, picks from your message list. not a bulk api blast.

🔁

moves on. never DMs the same person twice.

marks every follower as "seen" after sending. your existing followers are never touched.

how to install (2 min)

  1. download the zip
  2. unzip it anywhere on your computer
  3. go to chrome://extensions in your browser
  4. flip "developer mode" on (toggle in the top right)
  5. click "load unpacked" and pick the unzipped folder
  6. click the HB icon in your toolbar
  7. add 3 messages, set your daily limit, flip the toggle on
  8. leave an instagram tab open. that's it.
