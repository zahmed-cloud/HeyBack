Welcome to HeyBack

Auto-DMs new Instagram followers so you never miss saying hi.

1Type your welcome messages below (3-5 recommended)
2Set your daily limit (start at 10, keep it human)
3Flip the toggle ON. HeyBack does the rest.

We mark all your current followers as "seen" so only NEW followers get DMs.

Important

Instagram doesn't officially allow automation. HeyBack takes precautions (random delays, daily limits, human-like typing) but there's still risk.

We recommend using a secondary account, not your main. Keep daily limit at 10-15 for the first week.
